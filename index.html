WEBLOOPER v1.0

A lightweight, free web-based workstation designed for quick mobile rehearsals without the need for expensive audio interfaces or wired headphones. It utilizes a unique "spotlight" trick: a visual metronome guides your rhythm, and backing tracks automatically drop out for one cycle to ensure a clean microphone capture without bleed before the virtual band rejoins you.
